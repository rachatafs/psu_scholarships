{% trans "Guidance on resolving missing or unreadable application documents" %}

{% trans "Follow these steps to respond promptly" %}:
  1. {% trans "Check your PSU student email for the exact documents requested by the committee." %}
  2. {% trans "Prepare high-resolution, readable copies in PDF or JPG format." %}
  3. {% trans "Submit the additional files directly to the Student Affairs Office (Room 7102) or reply as instructed in the notification email." %}
  4. {% trans "Your application will remain in 'In Progress' status until the committee re-examines the new submissions." %}
